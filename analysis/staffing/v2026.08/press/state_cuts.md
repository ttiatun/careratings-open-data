# State cuts: nursing home staffing against the repealed federal minimums, release v2026.08

One section per state from the staffing-standards study (CMS Provider Information vintage 2026-08-01). Every figure is a column of `by_state.csv` or `state_change.csv`; ranks are among states with at least 10 certified nursing homes, highest share first. The floors are 0.55 registered-nurse, 2.45 nurse-aide and 3.48 total nurse hours per resident day, from the federal rule published in 2024 and repealed effective February 2, 2026 before any hour floor applied. Hours are as reported, not adjusted for resident acuity; a facility below a floor is not thereby found to be understaffed. The state minimum, where shown, is from `analysis/staffing/state_standards.json`; reported hours are not a state's compliance measure.
